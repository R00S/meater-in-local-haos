## Research notes

Native language: the everyday language of the Isle of Man is English; Manx Gaelic (Gaelg) is a
revived minority language and its food vocabulary is recorded in the notes below (Culture Vannin
Learn Manx: arran = bread, praase = potato, eayn = lamb, bainney = milk, caashey = cheese, feill =
meat, eeast = fish). Searches were run in English because that is the cuisine's living language;
Manx Gaelic terms were taken from Culture Vannin and the Manx Cookery Book.

**Primary historical source:** *The Manx Cookery Book of Favourite Dishes* (Morrison, 1908),
published for the Peel Church Spire fund — Manx and English recipes side by side
(isle-of-man.com/manxnotebook). Modern sources: Isle of Man Government (gov.im) fisheries and
agriculture pages, Culture Vannin, Isle of Man Creamery, Isle of Man Salt Co., Laxey Glen Mills,
Isle of Man Farmers' Markets, Visit Isle of Man, BBC Isle of Man (Sue King's *Ham and Egg and
Turtle Soup*, 2025), Bangor University Annual Fisheries Science Report 2023, GOV.UK protected
food-name register, RBST breed register.

**Two facts that shape every Bulk pair:**

1. **No Isle of Man per-capita food consumption statistics exist.** gov.im publishes agriculture
   and fisheries statistics but no food consumption survey; FAOSTAT has no separate Isle of Man
   series; DEFRA *Family Food* surveys the United Kingdom only (no Isle of Man sample). Bulk items
   therefore use **UK household-purchase proxies (DEFRA Family Food / AHDB / UK Food Security
   Report), explicitly labelled "UK proxy" in each note.** They are not Isle of Man measurements.
2. **Manx produce is only about 6% of all food consumed on the island** (Manx NFU via Energy FM
   Isle of Man) — the island imports the large majority of its food. Local-grade items are
   therefore the genuinely island-produced minority, and two Local pairs are documented as
   genuinely thin below.

**Verified Isle of Man figures used in notes:** ~3,500 tonnes of potatoes grown by Manx farmers a
year (Robinsons IOM / Manx NFU); Isle of Man Creamery (est. 1934, cooperative of 28 family farms)
processes over 26 million litres of milk a year; king scallop landings 1,816 t in the 2022/23
season (Bangor University); 62,769 t of sea fish landed into the Island 2011–2021 (draft Isle of
Man Fisheries Statement); Cornaa Fish Farm stocks 20,000 rainbow trout a year; queen scallop
2026 trawl-season TAC 1,350 tonnes (gov.im).

**Unresolved, deliberately omitted:** the 1908 book's *Bollan ny Houney* (Hollantide Eve supper of
salted fish with mashed potatoes and parsnips) names the fish only as "bollan"; the species could
not be confirmed from any source read here, so no entry was created for it.
