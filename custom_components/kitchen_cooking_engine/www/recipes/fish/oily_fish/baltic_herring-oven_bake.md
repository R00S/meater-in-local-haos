# Baltic Herring × Oven Bake — Recipe Temperature Research

## Cut profile

Oven-baking Baltic herring (strömming) centers on the "strömmingslåda" — the classic Swedish casserole where butterflied flundror are folded around a herb filling, set in a buttered dish, covered with seasoned cream (mustard cream or caviar cream) and baked at 225 °C for 15–30 minutes until the top is golden and the cream is set. The moist, cream-covered environment protects the lean, fine flesh; the casserole format also scales well for the julbord/smörgåsbord table. A simpler oven variant folds plain flundror and bakes them uncovered at 225 °C for about 10 minutes. All fetched sources state oven settings, times and visual endpoints (golden top, set cream); none states an internal pull temperature, and none is inferred here.

## Styckesprofil

Ugnsbakning av strömming kretsar kring strömmingslådan — fjärilsöppnade flundror med örtfyllning i smord form, täckta med smaksatt grädde (senaps- eller kaviargrädde) och gräddade vid 225 °C i 15–30 minuter tills toppen är gyllenbrun och grädden satt. Den fuktiga, gräddtäckta miljön skyddar det magra, fina köttet. En enklare variant gräddar nakna flundror i ugn vid 225 °C ca 10 minuter. Alla hämtade källor anger ugnstemperatur, tid och visuella slutpunkter; ingen anger innertemperatur.

## Source recipes

### 1. Köket.se — Strömmingslåda
**Inspired by**: https://www.koket.se/strommingslada (fetched 2026-10-23). Swedish recipe platform (TV4's Köket.se); no individual author named.
**Serves**: 4 · **Prep**: 15 min · **Cook**: 20–30 min at 225 °C · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 500 g strömmingsflundra (butterflied Baltic herring)
- 2 dl chopped parsley
- ½ small leek, chopped
- 2 dl whipping cream
- 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
- Breadcrumbs, to top
- Salt

**Method**
1. Heat the oven to 225 °C.
2. Lay out the strömming and salt it. Mix the parsley and leek, spread the filling over the fish, fold the fish over, and place in a buttered oven dish.
3. Lightly whip the cream, season with the mustard, and spread over the fish; scatter with breadcrumbs.
4. Bake in the centre of the oven 20–30 minutes. The source gives no internal temperature or explicit pull point; do not infer one from the stated time. The stated endpoint is the bake time and a finished surface.
5. Serve — the source states no rest or final serving temperature.

### 2. Köket.se — Strömmingslåda med kaviargrädde
**Inspired by**: https://www.koket.se/strommingslada-med-kaviargradde (fetched 2026-10-23). Swedish recipe platform (TV4's Köket.se); no individual author named.
**Serves**: 4 · **Prep**: not stated · **Cook**: 15–20 min at 225 °C · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 1 kg uncleaned strömming (or 600 g cleaned)
- Salt
- 1–1.5 dl chopped dill
- 1–1.5 dl chopped parsley
- 1–1.5 dl chopped chives
- 2 dl whipping cream
- ½–1 packet caviar
- Breadcrumbs

**Method**
1. Heat the oven to 225 °C. Clean the fish and remove the backbone. Lay the fillets flesh-side up on a board and salt them.
2. Butter an oven dish lightly. Mix the herbs and spread half in the bottom. Roll the fish and pack the rolls tightly in the dish; cover with the remaining herbs.
3. Whip the cream, season with caviar, and pour over the fish so it runs down between the rolls.
4. Bake in the centre of the oven 15–20 minutes; serve with boiled potatoes. The source gives no internal temperature or explicit pull point; do not infer one from the stated time.

### 3. Marthaförbundet (Martha.fi) — Strömmingsflundror (oven variant)
**Inspired by**: https://www.martha.fi/recept/strommingsflundror (fetched 2026-10-23). Finland-Swedish household association Marthaförbundet (Finnish organisation; recipe in Swedish). The same source also documents the pan-fry variant (see `baltic_herring-pan_fry.md`).
**Serves**: not stated (1 kg strömming / 600 g fillet) · **Prep**: not stated · **Cook**: about 10 min at 225 °C · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 1 kg strömming, or 600 g strömmingsfilé
- 1 dl rågmjöl (rye flour)
- Salt, white or black pepper
- Finely chopped dill and chives, or mint
- Butter (for the pan variant; paper-lined tray for the oven variant)

**Method**
1. Lay the fillets skin-side down on buttered or baking paper; season with salt and pepper.
2. Put the filling on half the fillets and pair the strömming two and two into flundror; dredge in rye flour.
3. Bake in the oven at 225 °C for about 10 minutes (the pan variant is 3–4 minutes per side in butter). The source gives no internal temperature or explicit pull point; do not infer one.
4. Serve with sour-cream sauce, boiled potatoes and raw cabbage with lingonberries. The source states no final serving temperature.

## What makes this method special

The strömmingslåda bakes the fish under a cream blanket: the casserole's moist environment keeps the lean flesh from drying while the exposed crumb-and-cream top gratinates, which is why a 225 °C oven can run 30 minutes on a fish that would be ruined by 10 minutes of dry heat. The rolled-flundra packing also protects the flesh. The uncovered variant is effectively a fast dry roast and takes a third of the time — the difference is entirely the protective medium.
