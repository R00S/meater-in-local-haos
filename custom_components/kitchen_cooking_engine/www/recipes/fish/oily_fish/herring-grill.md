# Herring × Grill — Recipe Temperature Research

## Cut profile

Grilling herring exploits the fish's naturally high fat content — the oil self-bastes the flesh over direct heat and drips to create aromatic smoke that flavours the skin. Herring are typically grilled whole (gutted, head on or off), scored 2–3 times per flank to prevent curling and let heat penetrate, or grilled as butterflied fillets. The fetched sources document three traditions: the British hot-grill (broiler) with compound mustard butter, Japanese whole-fish salt-grilling (*shioyaki*, 塩焼き), and Russian Don-region charcoal-grilling of fresh or thawed fish. All use qualitative endpoints; none states an internal pull temperature, and none is inferred here.

## Source recipes

### 1. Grilled herrings with mustard butter — BBC Good Food
**Inspired by**: https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/grilled-herrings-mustard-butter (recipe page verified 2026-05-26 by earlier session; magazine recipe, September 2005).
**Serves**: 4 · **Prep**: 10 min · **Cook**: 8 min · **Rest**: 2 min

**Ingredients**
- 4 whole herring, gutted and cleaned
- 25 g softened butter
- 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper
- Lemon wedges, to serve

**Method**
1. Using a sharp knife, slash each herring 2–3 times on each side.
2. Mix together the butter, mustard and parsley to make a compound butter.
3. Season the fish inside and out with salt and pepper.
4. Cook under a preheated hot grill (or over charcoal) for 3–4 minutes each side until the skin is blistered and charred in places.
5. The stated endpoint is opaque flesh and crisp skin. The source does not specify an internal temperature or explicit pull point; do not infer one.
6. Rest 2 minutes. Top with a slice of compound mustard butter and serve with lemon wedges.

### 2. cocomin23 / Cookpad JP — ニシンの姿焼き（塩焼き）(Whole grilled salted herring)
**Inspired by**: https://cookpad.com/jp/recipes/19484914 (fetched 2026-10-23). Japanese community recipe platform; contributor recipe by cocomin23 (whole 35 cm seasonal herring, salt-grilled).
**Serves**: 2 (2 herring) · **Prep**: 10 min+ salting · **Cook**: appliance grill, auto setting · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 2 herring (*nishiin*)
- Coarse salt (*arajio*), to taste
- 2 shiso leaves
- Grated daikon radish, to serve

**Method**
1. Scale the herring (a bottle cap works well), tail toward head; rinse and pat dry.
2. Salt the fish and leave 10+ minutes; wipe off the moisture that rises. Score both flesh sides, salt the fins ("cosmetic salt"), and salt the whole fish; grill on the appliance grill (*grill* function, auto setting).
3. Serve with shiso and grated daikon.
4. The source notes grill time varies by appliance, and gives no internal temperature or explicit pull point; do not infer one. Placing the head toward the back of the grill and the tail toward the front colours the fish evenly.

### 3. ЮсяЮся / EdimDoma — Сельдь гриль (charcoal-grilled fresh fish; herring variant)
**Inspired by**: https://www.edimdoma.ru/retsepty/30316-seld-gril.amp (fetched 2026-10-24). User recipe by ЮсяЮся; describes fresh or frozen herring and a charcoal-grill preparation.

**Ingredients**

**Method**
1. Wash and clean the fish; marinate if desired, or leave un-marinated.
2. Place on the grill grate over coals and grill. The source gives no duration or measured doneness endpoint.

## What makes this method special

Direct radiant heat renders herring's abundant subcutaneous fat as it cooks, effectively basting the flesh while dripping oil flares into the smoke that flavours the skin — a self-reinforcing cycle that suits this oily fish better than lean species. Scoring is structural: without it the skin contracts and curls the fish away from the heat. The Japanese salt-grill variant uses pre-salting and standing time to draw surface moisture before grilling; the Don-region recipe accepts optional marinading or plain fish and uses coals.
