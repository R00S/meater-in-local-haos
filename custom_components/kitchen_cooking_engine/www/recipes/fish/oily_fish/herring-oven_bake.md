# Herring × Oven Bake — Recipe Temperature Research

## Cut profile

Oven-baking herring is well-suited to the "rolled" preparation where butterflied fillets are stuffed with a flavouring (mustard, herbs, pancetta) and rolled, then baked in a hot oven. The high oven temperature (200–220 °C) crisps the outer surface while the interior steams in its own fat; cooking time is short (15–20 minutes), reflecting the small size and high fat content. Oven baking is less common than pan-frying for herring in Nordic cuisines, but is the convenient rolled-fillet method for domestic cooking in British and Scandinavian traditions. The fetched sources give oven settings, times and visual endpoints; none states an internal pull temperature, and none is inferred here.

### 1. Herrings rolled with mustard and pancetta — BBC Good Food
**Inspired by**: https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/herrings-rolled-mustard-pancetta (recipe page verified 2026-05-26 by earlier session; magazine recipe, October 2008).
**Serves**: 4 · **Prep**: 15 min · **Cook**: 15–20 min · **Rest**: 3 min

**Ingredients**
- 4 herrings, butterflied (heads removed, backbone taken out)
- 1 tbsp wholegrain mustard
- 4 slices pancetta or thin-cut streaky bacon
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh dill
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper
- Lemon, to serve

**Method**
1. Preheat oven to **220 °C / 200 °C fan / gas 7**.
2. Open each butterflied herring flat on a board. Season inside with salt and pepper.
3. Spread a thin layer of wholegrain mustard over the flesh side of each herring.
4. Lay a slice of pancetta on the flesh side and scatter over the dill.
5. Roll each herring from head to tail, securing with a cocktail stick if needed.
6. Place rolled herrings in a lightly oiled ovenproof dish.
7. Bake in the preheated oven for **15–20 minutes** until the pancetta is crispy and the herring is cooked through.
8. The stated endpoint is a set exterior and crisp pancetta. The source does not specify an internal temperature or explicit pull point; do not infer one.
9. Rest 3 minutes. Remove cocktail sticks and serve with lemon.

### 2. Sindirella / EdimDoma — Сельдь, запечённая в вине с травами и чесноком
**Inspired by**: https://www.edimdoma.ru/retsepty/19277-seld-zapechennaya-v-vine-s-travami-i-chesnokom.amp (fetched 2026-10-24). User recipe by Sindirella; serves two.
**Serves**: 2 · **Prep**: not stated · **Cook**: 30 min · **Rest**: not stated; served hot

**Ingredients**
- 2 fresh-frozen, unsalted herring, 250–300 g each
- 1 bay leaf
- 2 sprigs fresh thyme
- 1 tbsp mustard
- 200 ml dry white wine
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp chopped parsley
- Salt and freshly ground pepper
- Butter for the baking tray

**Method**
1. Heat the oven to **200 °C**.
2. Remove heads and innards; wash, then season inside and outside with salt and pepper.
3. Butter a baking tray. Put half a bay leaf and a thyme sprig inside each fish and place the fish on the tray.
4. Mix white wine with mustard and pour into the tray. Bake for **15 minutes**.
5. Remove bay and thyme, scatter parsley mixed with garlic on top, and bake for another **15 minutes**.
6. Serve hot. The source does not state an internal temperature or explicit doneness test.

### 3. James Beard Foundation — Baked Spiced Herring
**Inspired by**: https://www.jamesbeard.org/recipes/baked-spiced-herring (fetched 2026-10-24). Published recipe from the James Beard Foundation; yields six servings.
**Serves**: 6 · **Prep**: simmer vegetables about 20 min · **Cook**: 10 min per inch of fish thickness · **Rest**: cool fish in cooking liquid; serve chilled

**Ingredients**
- Butter
- 3 carrots, chopped
- 3 onions, chopped
- 1 garlic clove, chopped
- 4 parsley sprigs, chopped
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper
- White wine, to cover
- 1 bay leaf, thyme sprig, 6 peppercorns, pinch allspice, 2 cloves
- 12 small herrings
- Onion rings and thin carrot slices
- 1/4 cup wine vinegar

**Method**
1. Preheat oven to **425 °F / 218 °C**. Butter a large baking dish and cover the base with chopped carrots, onions, garlic, and parsley; season, add wine to cover and the herbs/spices.
2. Bring the vegetables and liquid to a boil, then simmer until tender, about **20 minutes**.
3. Clean the fish and place it on the vegetables; top with onion rings and carrot slices. Add vinegar and enough wine to cover.
4. Bake for **10 minutes per inch** measured at the fish's thickest point.
5. Cool the fish in the liquid and serve chilled. The source gives no core temperature.

## What makes this method special

The rolled format gives herring two protective layers (its own skin-out roll plus pancetta) while the hot oven crisps the exposed fat — the interior effectively steams in rendered herring oil, which is why a 220 °C oven cannot dry it the way it would a lean fish. The method trades the pan's contact crust for an evenly crisped wrap and hands-off scaling for a crowd.
