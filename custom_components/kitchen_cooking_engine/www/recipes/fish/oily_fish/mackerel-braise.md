# Mackerel × Braise — Recipe Temperature Research

## Cut profile

Korean mackerel braises layer raw fish with radish, onion and a small amount of seasoned liquid, then bring the pot to a boil and reduce to a covered simmer. This low-liquid, vegetable-supported cooking differs from plain broth simmering and does not sear the fish first. The Atlantic mackerel recipe below explicitly identifies the fish species; it gives no internal-temperature endpoint.

## Source recipes

### 1. Imsun and Emily Miller / Fishful Future — Korean Spicy Braised Mackerel
**Inspired by**: https://www.fishfulfuture.com/food-from-the-sea-comida-del-mar/imsun-eng (fetched 2026-10-24). Korean tradition; authors identify the fish as Atlantic mackerel and cut the whole fish into 3-inch steaks, then halve along the backbone.

**Serves**: 4 · **Prep**: not stated · **Cook**: 15 min at low simmer · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 2 large whole Atlantic mackerel; 1 large daikon radish; 1 onion; 1 bundle green onions; optional bundle red Korean chiles; 1 cup water
- Seasoning: 1/2 cup soy sauce; 2 tbsp sesame oil; 1 tbsp gochujang; 2 tbsp white sugar; 4 tbsp gochugaru; 5 large garlic cloves; 1 thumb ginger

**Method**
1. Mince garlic and ginger and mix with seasoning ingredients. Slice onion and green onions; slice peeled daikon.
2. Clean fish, remove fins and optionally heads, gut, cut into 3-inch steaks and halve along backbone.
3. Layer onion and daikon in a deep pot, add fish and cover with seasoning. Add green onion and optional chiles.
4. Add 1 cup water, bring to a boil, then reduce to a low simmer for 15 minutes or until fully cooked.
5. Serve hot with rice.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step; **no internal temperature stated**. Endpoint is 15 minutes at low simmer or fully cooked.

### 2. Hyegyoung K. Ford / Beyond Kimchee — Korean Braised Mackerel and Radish
**Inspired by**: https://www.beyondkimchee.com/braised-korean-mackerel/ (fetched 2026-10-24). Korean tradition; ingredient line: “1 whole mackerel fish, well-cleaned and cut into 3–4 pieces.” Species not stated.

**Serves**: 3 · **Prep**: 15 min (+10–15 min rice-water soak) · **Cook**: 20 min · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 1 whole mackerel, cleaned and cut into 3–4 pieces; 3 cups rice water; 10 oz Korean radish; 1/2 large onion; 1 optional fresh chile; 1 green onion
- Sauce: 2 tbsp gochugaru; 3 tbsp soy sauce; 1 tbsp Korean tuna sauce; 2 garlic cloves; 2 tsp ginger paste; 1/2 tsp sugar; 1/2 tsp pepper; 1/2 cup water

**Method**
1. Soak fish pieces in rice-starch water 10–15 minutes; drain.
2. Mix gochugaru, soy sauce, tuna sauce, garlic, ginger, sugar and pepper.
3. Layer onion and radish in a shallow braising pot; spread half the sauce over radish. Add fish and remaining sauce.
4. Add 1/2 cup water, cover, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low and simmer 15–20 minutes until radish softens.
5. Garnish with chopped green onion and serve warm with rice.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step; **no internal temperature stated**. Endpoint is soft radish and 15–20 minutes.
