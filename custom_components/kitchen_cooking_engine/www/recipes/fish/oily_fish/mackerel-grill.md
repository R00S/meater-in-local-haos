# Mackerel × Grill — Recipe Temperature Research

## Cut profile

Grilling mackerel exposes its oily flesh to direct radiant heat, so the fish cooks quickly and benefits from a basket or careful handling. Charcoal grilling is a documented fuel/setup variant of the grill method rather than a separate enum method: its coals provide the heat source, while the same fish is cooked over a grate and turned. The sources use visual doneness cues and do not state internal temperatures.

## Source recipes

### 1. Imma / Immaculate Bites — Whole Grilled Mackerel
**Inspired by**: https://www.africanbites.com/whole-grilled-mackerel/ (fetched 2026-10-24). Fusion/African-diaspora home cooking; ingredient line: 2–3 whole mackerel, about 1.5–2 lb each.

**Serves**: 4 · **Prep**: 15 min (+30 min minimum marinating) · **Cook**: about 14+ min · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 2–3 whole mackerel, about 1.5–2 lb each; salt and pepper; 3–4 fresh lemons
- 2 tbsp minced garlic; 1–1.5 tbsp minced ginger; 1–2 tsp minced basil
- Optional: 2 tsp or more chicken bouillon powder or Maggie

**Method**
1. Rinse and dry fish. Make 3–4 diagonal cuts on each side through the bone; season inside and outside with salt, pepper and lemon juice.
2. Mix garlic, ginger and basil; coat fish inside and out. Marinate at least 30 minutes or up to 24 hours.
3. Oil grill basket. For charcoal, open bottom vents, add charcoal about 1/4 full, and wait until flames die down and ash turns white.
4. Grill about **7 or more minutes per side**, flipping as needed. Baste with unused marinade during the final 5 minutes if desired.
5. Check doneness by ensuring flesh nearest the bone is fully cooked; the author suggests slashing with a knife to inspect.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step; **no internal temperature stated**. Endpoint is fully cooked flesh closest to bone.

### 2. Epicurious — Grilled Whole Mackerel with Lemon, Oregano, and Olives
**Inspired by**: https://www.epicurious.com/recipes/food/views/grilled-whole-mackerel-with-lemon-oregano-and-olives-109523 (fetched 2026-10-24). US recipe; exact cut: 1 whole 3 1/4–3 1/2 lb Spanish mackerel or bluefish, cleaned, head and tail on. Species is Spanish mackerel, not Atlantic/Indian; included only as a method lead and not claimed as same species evidence.

**Serves**: 4 · **Prep**: 45 min active · **Cook**: about 30 min · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 1/2 tsp lemon zest; 1 1/2 tbsp lemon juice; 1/3 cup olive oil
- 1/4 cup pitted Kalamata olives; 3 tbsp chopped oregano plus 6 large sprigs
- 1 whole 3 1/4–3 1/2 lb Spanish mackerel or bluefish, cleaned
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil; 6 lemon slices

**Method**
1. Whisk lemon zest and juice with salt and pepper; whisk in olive oil and olives and chopped oregano.
2. Slash fish at 2-inch intervals, oil and season it; place lemon slices and oregano in cavity and tie closed.
3. For charcoal, open bottom vents and light coals; medium-hot is defined as holding a hand 5 inches above the rack for 3–4 seconds.
4. Grill covered only for gas grills for 15 minutes; turn fish and grill about 15 minutes more until just cooked through.
5. Serve with the lemon-olive sauce.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step; **no internal temperature stated**. The page says cook until just cooked through.

### 3. Pakus / Directo al Paladar — Caballa a la Barbacoa
**Inspired by**: https://www.directoalpaladar.com/recetas-de-pescados-y-mariscos/receta-caballa-a-barbacoa-ideal-para-disfrutar-pescado-a-brasas-verano (fetched 2026-10-24). Spanish tradition; whole mackerel about 450 g.

**Serves**: 2 · **Prep**: 3 min · **Cook**: 12 min · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 1 whole mackerel, about 450 g; salt and black pepper; olive oil for brushing

**Method**
1. Clean fish, remove innards if needed, season inside and brush with a little olive oil.
2. Prepare barbecue coals; once grey and ready, place fish about 10 cm above coals.
3. Cook about 5–6 minutes per side, turning carefully with barbecue tongs.
4. Add salt crystals at the table.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step; **no internal temperature stated**.

### 4. Korean Bapsang — Godeungeo Gui (Grilled Mackerel)
**Inspired by**: https://www.koreanbapsang.com/godengeo-gui-grilled-mackerel/ (fetched 2026-10-24). Korean tradition; fresh mackerel filleted or butterflied whole.

**Serves**: 2 · **Prep**: 10 min (+20–30 min salting) · **Cook**: 6 min · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 1 fresh mackerel; salt; optional 1 tbsp lemon juice and 1 tsp ginger juice

**Method**
1. Score skin, pat dry and add optional lemon/ginger juices. Salt both sides and rest 20–30 minutes.
2. Shake off excess salt; clean and oil grill, preheat medium-high.
3. Grill skin-side down 2–3 minutes until edges are golden and flesh opaque; turn and cook about 2 minutes more.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step; **no internal temperature stated**.

## Temperature consensus

The four recipes use visual endpoints (fully cooked near bone, just cooked through, time over coals, or opaque flesh). None states a fish internal temperature. This leaf establishes charcoal and grill methods but not a numeric target.

## Summary

Whole mackerel grilling is documented in African-diaspora, US, Spanish and Korean sources, with charcoal clearly named in two. The Epicurious recipe uses Spanish mackerel, so it is a method lead rather than species-equivalent Atlantic/Indian evidence. **PARTIAL RESEARCH**: source integrity is verified, but source-specific species diversity and full method-analysis gates remain incomplete.
