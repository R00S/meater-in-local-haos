# Mackerel × Oven Broil — Recipe Temperature Research

## Cut profile

Oven broiling applies strong radiant heat from above and cooks mackerel quickly, usually skin-side down on a tray close to the element. The fish's oil and skin tolerate high top heat, while the short cook helps preserve moist flesh; recipe endpoints are opaque or flaky rather than measured internal temperatures. This is a distinct oven method in the enum, not outdoor grilling.

## Source recipes

### 1. Bon Appétit — Broiled Mackerel with Scallions and Lemon
**Inspired by**: https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/broiled-mackerel-scallions-lemon (fetched 2026-10-24). US food-media recipe; mackerel fillets, species unspecified.

**Serves**: 4 · **Prep**: not stated · **Cook**: 6–8 min · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 1 1/2 lb mackerel fillets; 2 tbsp olive oil; kosher salt and black pepper
- 2 tsp finely grated lemon zest; 3 tbsp lemon juice; lemon wedges
- 3 tbsp chopped flat-leaf parsley; 2 tbsp chopped dill; 2 bunches scallions

**Method**
1. Heat broiler to high and line a rimmed baking sheet with foil.
2. Rub fish with 1 tbsp oil; season with salt and pepper. Place skin-side down and add lemon zest, juice, parsley and dill.
3. Toss scallions with remaining oil. Broil fish until just opaque in the center, **6–8 minutes**, adding scallions for the last 4 minutes.
4. Serve with scallions and lemon wedges.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step; **no internal temperature stated**. The page says broil until just opaque.

### 2. Seafood Nutrition Partnership / adapted from NOAA Chef Laura Rogers — Herb-Broiled Mackerel
**Inspired by**: https://www.seafoodnutrition.org/project/herb-broiled-mackerel/ (fetched 2026-10-24). US recipe adapted from NOAA Chef Laura Rogers; page describes U.S. wild-caught Atlantic mackerel as a sustainable choice but its ingredient line says “4 mackerel fillets.”

**Serves**: 4 · **Prep**: 5 min · **Cook**: 8–10 min · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 4 mackerel fillets
- 1/4 cup softened margarine or butter; 1 tbsp chopped fresh parsley; 1 tbsp thinly sliced green onion
- 1/2 tsp chopped fresh tarragon (or 1/4 tsp dried); 1/2 tsp fresh thyme (or 1/4 tsp dried)
- 1 tsp salt; 1/8 tsp freshly ground black pepper; 1/8 tsp paprika

**Method**
1. Place fillets skin-side down in a greased broiler-safe pan without a rack.
2. Blend butter with parsley, green onion, tarragon, thyme, salt, pepper and paprika; spread over fillets.
3. Broil about 4 inches from heat until fish flakes with a fork, about **8–10 minutes**, basting once or twice with pan juices.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step; **no internal temperature stated**. The page uses flaking and 8–10 minutes.

### 3. Namiko Hirasawa Chen / Just One Cookbook — Grilled Mackerel with Shio Koji (Broiler method)
**Inspired by**: https://www.justonecookbook.com/grilled-mackerel-with-shio-koji/ (fetched 2026-10-24). Japanese tradition; source's recommended method is oven broiling. Ingredient line: 5.3 oz mackerel (2 pieces), form not further specified.

**Serves**: 2 · **Prep**: 10 min · **Cook**: 15–20 min · **Rest**: marinate at least 30 min

**Ingredients**
- 5.3 oz mackerel (2 pieces); 2 tbsp shio koji (or 2 tsp kosher salt, with less umami); lemon wedges

**Method**
1. Marinate mackerel in shio koji at least 30 minutes.
2. Preheat broiler on medium/high with rack about 6 inches below the element for 3 minutes; line tray with foil and lightly oil it.
3. Place fish skin-side down and broil **15–20 minutes** until surface is blistered and browned; do not flip.
4. Serve immediately with lemon wedges.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step; **no internal temperature stated**. The source's endpoint is blistered, browned surface.
