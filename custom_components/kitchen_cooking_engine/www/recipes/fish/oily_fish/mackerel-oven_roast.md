# Mackerel × Oven Roast — Recipe Temperature Research

## Cut profile

Whole mackerel roasted in an oven is cooked by dry convective heat, often with herbs and vegetables or wrapped in foil. The fish's oil helps protect the flesh, but recipes emphasize avoiding overcooking and rely on opacity, flaking or a timed roast. Species varies in the sources: Atlantic mackerel, unspecified mackerel and Spanish mackerel are not interchangeable claims, so each is named as published.

## Source recipes

### 1. Camilla Lombard / Sea Forager — Roasted Mackerel with Garlic and Paprika
**Inspired by**: https://www.seaforager.com/recipes/roasted-mackerel-with-garlic-and-paprika (fetched 2026-10-24). Adapted from Gordon Ramsay’s *Home Cooking*; whole mackerel, species not stated.

**Serves**: not stated · **Prep**: not stated · **Cook**: 25 min · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 2 garlic cloves; 2 tsp paprika; 1 tsp sea salt, plus more; olive oil
- 4–5 whole gutted mackerel; 1 lb new potatoes; 2–3 scallions; 2 lemons
- Vinaigrette: pinch of saffron; 1 tbsp white-wine vinegar; 1 tsp Dijon mustard; 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil; juice of 1/2 lemon; salt and black pepper

**Method**
1. Preheat oven to **450°F**.
2. Pound garlic, paprika and salt to a paste; loosen with a few drops of olive oil and rub inside and outside of fish.
3. Whisk vinaigrette ingredients together.
4. Oil a baking sheet, lay fish on it, put two thin lemon slices inside each fish and season skin with salt.
5. Roast uncovered **25 minutes** until skin is golden brown and meat by the “neck” is opaque.
6. Boil potatoes separately 15 minutes; lightly crush with olive oil and scallions. Serve fish and potatoes with vinaigrette.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe; **no internal temperature stated**. Endpoint is golden skin and opaque flesh by the neck.

### 2. Suzy Karadsheh / The Mediterranean Dish — Mediterranean Oven-Roasted Spanish Mackerel
**Inspired by**: https://www.themediterraneandish.com/oven-roasted-spanish-mackerel/ (fetched 2026-10-24). Mediterranean/Egyptian family context; exact cut: 1.5 lb whole Spanish mackerel, cleaned and gutted, head retained.

**Serves**: not stated · **Prep**: 10 min · **Cook**: 30–40 min (recipe card says 30 min; instructions vary by thickness) · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 3 large garlic cloves; 1 tsp salt; 1 tsp ground coriander; 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/4 cup finely chopped parsley; 1/4 cup finely chopped dill
- 1.5 lb whole Spanish mackerel; 1 lemon, thinly sliced; extra-virgin olive oil; 2–3 tbsp lemon juice
- Optional sides: salad or vegetables, tahini sauce, Lebanese rice

**Method**
1. Preheat oven to **350°F** and place a sheet pan inside to warm.
2. Crush garlic with salt, coriander and cumin; mix in parsley and dill.
3. Dry fish, remove dark cavity vein if present, season inside and out, and place on foil large enough to enclose it.
4. Stuff cavity with lemon slices and herb mixture; drizzle with olive oil and seal foil tightly.
5. Bake on the hot sheet pan at **350°F for 30–40 minutes**, depending on thickness.
6. Add lemon juice and serve immediately.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step; **no internal temperature stated**. Time varies by fish thickness; source says bake in sealed foil.

### 3. Diane Kochilas / The Ikaria Diet — Whole Mackerel Baked over Vegetables
**Inspired by**: https://www.dianekochilas.com/whole-mackerel-baked-over-vegetables/ (fetched 2026-10-24). Greek-inspired; exact cut: whole mackerel about 2 lb, cleaned with head and tail intact; species not stated.

**Serves**: 6 · **Prep**: 15 min · **Cook**: 20–25 min · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 1 whole mackerel, about 2 lb; 1 tsp dried Greek oregano; 1 lemon, sliced
- Red onion, coarsely chopped (quantity not stated); 1 cup pitted Kalamata olives; 2 cups cherry tomatoes
- 10 sprigs flat-leaf parsley; Greek olive oil for drizzling; sea salt and freshly ground pepper

**Method**
1. Preheat oven to **425°F / 220°C** and line pan with parchment. Arrange onion, tomatoes and olives; drizzle oil and season lightly.
2. Score fish skin on both sides and place over vegetables.
3. Oil and season fish; add oregano, lemon slices and parsley in and over cavity.
4. Roast until opaque and cooked through, **20–25 minutes**.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step; **no internal temperature stated**.

### 4. Stefan / Stefan’s Gourmet Blog — Roasted Mackerel (Sgombro al Forno)
**Inspired by**: https://stefangourmet.com/2014/03/03/roasted-mackerel-sgombro-al-forno/ (fetched 2026-10-24). Italian-inspired; exact cut: 1 fresh mackerel, about 400 g, gutted; species not stated.

**Serves**: 1 · **Prep**: not stated · **Cook**: 15 min · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 1 fresh mackerel, about 400 g, gutted
- 2 baby carrots; handful cherry tomatoes; small handful black pitted olives; 1 rosemary sprig; 1 garlic clove; 2 thyme sprigs
- Lemon wedges; extra-virgin olive oil; salt and freshly ground black pepper; pinch of sugar; 1/2 tbsp minced flat-leaf parsley

**Method**
1. Cut carrots into 5 cm lengths and parboil in salted water with a pinch of sugar for 10 minutes; drain and rub off skin.
2. Toss tomatoes, carrots and olives with olive oil in a baking dish. Preheat oven to **225°C / 440°F**.
3. Stuff fish cavity with rosemary, thyme and garlic; rub with olive oil and season.
4. Place in baking dish and roast at **225°C / 440°F for about 15 minutes**, or until just cooked. The author warns not to overcook.
5. Serve with vegetables, lemon and parsley.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe; **no internal temperature stated**.

## Temperature consensus

The four sources use oven settings of 350°F, 425°F/220°C, 440°F/225°C and 450°F; cook time varies from 15 to 40 minutes with fish size and foil/vegetable setup. All use qualitative endpoints and state no internal fish temperature. The 4-source count is met, but species and national-tradition distinctions require review: one source explicitly uses Spanish mackerel, while others do not specify species.
