# Mackerel × Pan Sear — Recipe Temperature Research

## Cut profile

Pan-searing mackerel uses a hot skillet and a short contact cook to brown the surface, often followed by a brief residual-heat finish rather than a long shallow-fry. The retrieved named recipe applies this to Spanish mackerel fillets and turns off the heat before flipping, so the fish finishes gently. Species-specific evidence for Atlantic/Indian mackerel remains thinner than the general mackerel pan-fry collection.

## Source recipe

### 1. Steven Satterfield / James Beard Foundation — Sautéed Spanish Mackerel with Celery Salsa Verde
**Inspired by**: https://www.jamesbeard.org/recipes/sauted-spanish-mackerel-with-celery-salsa-verde (fetched 2026-10-24). US chef recipe; specifically Spanish mackerel, not Atlantic/Indian mackerel.

**Serves**: 2 · **Prep**: not stated · **Cook**: brief skillet sear plus 30 sec rest in pan · **Rest**: 30 sec in pan

**Ingredients**
- Salsa verde: 1 small garlic clove; zest of 1 lemon; 1 tsp Dijon mustard; 1/2 tsp kosher salt; 1/4 cup chopped celery leaves; 2 tbsp chopped mint; 2 tbsp chopped parsley; 1/3 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 medium Spanish mackerel fillets (5–6 oz each), skin removed; kosher salt; freshly ground black pepper; 2 tsp extra-virgin olive oil; 1 lemon wedge

**Method**
1. Combine garlic, lemon zest, mustard and salt; stir in celery leaves, mint, parsley and olive oil for salsa verde.
2. Pat fillets dry and season both sides with salt and pepper.
3. Warm olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Lay fillets in hot oil and lightly brown one side.
4. Turn off the heat. When ready to serve, flip fillets and let sit in the pan 30 seconds; transfer to plates, finish with lemon and salsa verde.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step; **no internal temperature stated**. The page gives no exact sear time, only lightly browned on one side and 30 seconds after flipping with heat off.
