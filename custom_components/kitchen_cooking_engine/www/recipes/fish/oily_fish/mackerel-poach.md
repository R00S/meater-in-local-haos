# Mackerel × Poach — Recipe Temperature Research

## Cut profile

Poaching mackerel submerges whole fish or fillets in liquid held just below a simmer, or cooks the fish gently in a shallow amount of liquid in a warm oven. The low agitation protects the oily fish's tender flesh from breaking apart; sources vary from a brief court-bouillon poach to oil poaching and a covered oven method. Most pages do not state internal temperature, and a generic article's separate temperature advice is not treated as a recipe-specific target.

## Source recipes

### 1. Donna Deane / Los Angeles Times — Poached Mackerel with Ginger-Soy Sauce and Soba Noodles
**Inspired by**: https://www.latimes.com/recipe/poached-mackerel-with-ginger-soy-sauce-and-soba-noodles (fetched 2026-10-24). Japanese-style; exact cut: “2 whole mackerel, cleaned, heads removed, tails left on (about 1 pound),” cut lengthwise along the spine. Species not stated.

**Serves**: not stated · **Prep**: not stated · **Cook**: aromatics simmer 15 min; fish poach 5–7 min · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 2 whole mackerel, cleaned, heads removed, tails left on (about 1 lb)
- Dashi-based broth, fresh ginger and green onion; individual quantities not available in the fetched recipe extract
- Dressing/serving components named in title: ginger-soy sauce and soba noodles; quantities not available in extract

**Method**
1. Prepare a dashi broth with fresh ginger and green onion; simmer aromatics about 15 minutes before adding fish.
2. Add fish, lower heat until liquid is just below simmer with no bubbles breaking the surface, cover and poach 5–7 minutes.
3. Serve dressed with loose green tea and cold soba noodles as described in the page text.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step. The article gives general fish advice of “At 160 degrees, it’s done” without a unit; this is not a recipe-specific instruction, so record **no internal temperature stated in the recipe**.

### 2. Darina Allen / Irish Examiner — Warm Poached Mackerel with Bretonne Sauce
**Inspired by**: https://www.irishexaminer.com/food/arid-40032983.html (fetched 2026-10-24). Irish tradition; exact cut: “4 fresh mackerel,” cleaned and kept whole. Species not stated.

**Serves**: not stated · **Prep**: not stated · **Cook**: about 5–8 min off heat (recipe card lists 10 min) · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 4 fresh whole mackerel; water 1.2 L; salt
- Bretonne sauce ingredients are in the page but not fully reproduced here because this leaf concerns the fish poaching stage; sauce uses butter and is prepared separately

**Method**
1. Bring 1.2 L salted water to a boil.
2. Add cleaned whole fish and bring back to boiling point, then remove from heat.
3. Check after 5–8 minutes off heat; flesh should lift from the bone.
4. Serve warm with Bretonne sauce.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step; **no internal temperature stated**. Time and flesh lifting from the bone are the published cues.

### 4. Province of Newfoundland and Labrador / Seafood from Canada — Atlantic Mackerel with Lemon Stuffing
**Inspired by**: https://seafoodfromcanada.ca/recipe/mackerel-with-lemon-stuffing/ (fetched 2026-10-24). Atlantic Canadian; page title explicitly identifies Atlantic mackerel and credits the recipe to the Province of Newfoundland and Labrador, not an individual author.

**Serves**: 4 · **Prep**: not stated · **Cook**: 15–20 min in warm oven · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 4 × 6 oz (170 g) whole mackerel, split and boned; juice of 1/2 lemon; salt and black pepper, pinch; 1 crumbled bay leaf
- Stuffing: 1 tbsp olive oil or unsalted butter; 1 small onion, finely chopped; 3 oz (85 g) fresh white breadcrumbs; zest and juice of 1 lemon; 1 tbsp Mount Scio savory; 1/2 beaten egg

**Method**
1. Fry onion in oil or butter over medium-high heat 5 minutes until golden; stir in savory for 1 minute. Combine with remaining stuffing ingredients.
2. Lay fish skin-side down, season with lemon juice, salt and pepper; spoon stuffing onto one side, fold over and reshape.
3. Place in ovenproof casserole, barely cover dish bottom with water and add bay leaf.
4. Cover with lid, parchment or foil and poach in a warm oven at **350 degrees** for 15–20 minutes, until tender when pierced with a skewer.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step; **no internal temperature stated**. Page prints 350 degrees without a unit; do not infer one.

### 5. Connie Veneracion / Umami Days — Mackerel with Lemon Butter Sauce (Oil-Poached)
**Inspired by**: https://cooking.umamidays.com/mackerel-with-lemon-butter-sauce/ (fetched 2026-10-24). Filipino author; species not stated. Exact cut: 2–4 whole scaled and gutted mackerel, scored.

**Serves**: not stated · **Prep**: not stated · **Cook**: 10 min per side · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 2–4 whole mackerel, scaled, gutted and scored
- Olive oil for poaching; lemon-butter sauce prepared separately (quantities not in fetched extract)

**Method**
1. Heat olive oil to **160–180°F**.
2. Lower whole fish into oil and poach 10 minutes per side (about 20 minutes total).
3. Serve with lemon-butter sauce.

**Probe and endpoint**: no fish probe step; **no internal temperature stated**. 160–180°F is the oil-bath temperature, not an internal fish target.
