# Mackerel × Sauté — Recipe Temperature Research

## Cut profile

A French sauté is a short, uncovered cook in a small amount of hot fat, turning the food once or tossing it to brown and finish through; it is distinct from a high-heat initial sear followed by another cooking method. Darina Allen's named cookbook recipe explicitly fillets the mackerel and cooks it in a single layer in butter until golden on both sides, then makes a mushroom-herb accompaniment in the same pan. This is direct evidence for the `saute` enum, although it does not state a temperature or an exact cook time and the 4–6-source diversity gate is unmet.

## Method distinction and evidence limits
