## Source recipe lead (not counted as a slow-cooked mackerel method)

### Russell Hobbs UK — Mackerel, Tomato and Basil
**Inspired by**: https://ie.russellhobbs.com/recipes/cuisines/mackerel-tomato-and-basil (fetched 2026-10-24). UK appliance-brand recipe; ingredient list includes “6 mackerel fillets / 3 whole mackerel.” Author not named.

**Ingredients**
- 6 mackerel fillets or 3 whole mackerel
- Tomato sauce: 2 onions, finely diced; 6 garlic cloves; 80 g tomato purée; 5 g paprika; 4 × 400 g tins chopped tomatoes; 1 bunch fresh basil; salt and pepper

**Method as published**
1. Put tomato sauce ingredients except basil in the slow-cooker pot and cook on the highest setting for 4–5 hours; stir in basil before serving sauce with mackerel.
2. Preheat grill to setting 5. Rub mackerel with a little oil, season and grill skin-side down; turn whole fish once skin is crisp. Cook until fish starts to flake.

**Classification**: the mackerel is grilled, not slow-cooked; the slow cooker is only used for sauce. No mackerel internal temperature is stated.
