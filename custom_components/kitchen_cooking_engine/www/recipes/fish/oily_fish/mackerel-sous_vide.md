# Mackerel × Sous Vide — Recipe Temperature Research

## Cut profile

Sous vide cooks mackerel in a sealed bag in a controlled water bath, followed in these recipes by a short skin-side sear. Mackerel fillets are thin and oily, so the bath gives a repeatable texture while limiting direct heat exposure; the brief sear restores crisp skin. The published bath setpoints vary from 50°C to 122°F (about 50°C), and these sources do not state a fish internal temperature distinct from the bath.

## Source recipes

### 1. Sharon Chen / StreetSmart Kitchen — Sous Vide Mackerel with Spicy Cilantro Lime Dressing
**Inspired by**: https://www.streetsmartkitchen.com/sous-vide-mackerel-recipe/ (fetched 2026-10-24). Contemporary US home cooking; ingredient line: “4 mackerel fillets, defrosted.” Species not stated.

**Serves**: 4 · **Prep**: not stated · **Cook**: 20 min bath + 30 sec sear · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 4 defrosted mackerel fillets; kosher salt; freshly ground black pepper
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- Dressing: 1 cup fresh cilantro; 1 jalapeño; 2 garlic cloves; 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil; juice of 2 limes (about 1/4 cup); 1 tbsp coconut aminos

**Method**
1. Preheat water bath to **122°F**.
2. Rinse and dry fillets; season both sides with salt and pepper. Seal in one layer in a sous-vide bag.
3. Cook submerged in the bath for **20 minutes**.
4. Blend dressing ingredients. Remove fish, pat dry, and sear skin-side down in hot oil about **30 seconds** until crisp; serve with dressing.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step; **no internal temperature stated**. 122°F is the published water-bath setpoint.

### 2. Great British Chefs — How to Cook Mackerel Sous Vide
**Inspired by**: https://www.greatbritishchefs.com/how-to-cook/how-to-cook-mackerel-sous-vide (fetched 2026-10-24). British food-media recipe; individual author not credited. Ingredient line: “2 mackerel fillets, pinboned.” Species not stated.

**Serves**: not stated · **Prep**: not stated · **Cook**: 20 min bath + 1–2 min sear · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 2 pinboned mackerel fillets; salt; a dash of oil for searing
- Optional flavourings listed: chilli oil or garlic oil, black pepper or lemon rind

**Method**
1. Preheat water bath to **50°C**.
2. Season fillets with sea salt and seal under full pressure.
3. Cook in the bath for **20 minutes**.
4. Remove from bag and pat dry. Heat a frying pan over high heat with a dash of oil.
5. Sear skin until crisp, **1–2 minutes**, and serve immediately.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step; **no internal temperature stated**. 50°C is the water-bath setpoint.

### 3. Stefan / Stefan’s Gourmet Blog — Venetian Marinated Mackerel “in Saor” Sous Vide
**Inspired by**: https://stefangourmet.com/2012/04/01/venetian-marinated-mackerel-in-saor-sous-vide/ (fetched 2026-10-24). Venetian *pesce in saor*; ingredient line: “Fillets of 2 fresh mackerels, about 400 grams”; author says fillet and skin the fish. Species not stated.

**Serves**: 4 as antipasti · **Prep**: not stated · **Cook**: 15 min bath (plus marinade preparation) · **Rest**: marinate 6–24 h; bring to room temperature about 1 h before serving

**Ingredients**
- About 400 g fillets from 2 fresh mackerel; 2 large onions
- 300 ml dry white wine; 120 ml white-wine vinegar; 2 tbsp olive oil
- 4 tbsp white raisins; 2 tbsp pine nuts; 1 bay leaf
- Ground cinnamon; salt; freshly ground white pepper; 1 lemon; flat-leaf parsley

**Method**
1. Soak raisins 10 minutes; roast pine nuts 10 minutes at 150°C / 300°F. Slice onions.
2. Cook onions in olive oil over low heat until golden, about 10 minutes. Add vinegar, wine, raisins, pine nuts and bay leaf; simmer 15 minutes. Season and cool.
3. Remove the central bones from the fillets, season with salt and white pepper, seal in a bag and cook sous vide **15 minutes at 50°C / 122°F**. Cool in ice water.
4. Layer fish and onion marinade in a dish, add remaining marinade liquid and parsley; cover and refrigerate 6–24 hours.
5. Remove from refrigerator about an hour before serving, and serve with lemon.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step; **no internal temperature stated**. 50°C / 122°F is the bath setting; 150°C / 300°F applies only to roasting pine nuts.

### 4. Per Asmussen / GastroFun — Mackerel Sous Vide
**Inspired by**: https://gastrofun.net/recipe/mackerel-sous-vide/ (fetched 2026-10-24). Danish cuisine; ingredient line: “1 pcs. Mackerel or fillets.” Species not stated.

**Serves**: 2 · **Prep**: 15 min · **Cook**: 25 min card time (20 min bath, 1–2 min sear) · **Rest**: about 10 min salt cure

**Ingredients**
- 1 mackerel or fillets; coarse salt; ghee or oil/butter

**Method**
1. Fillet the mackerel if needed; remove scales and central bones.
2. Salt both sides and leave about **10 minutes**; rinse under cold water.
3. Vacuum-pack and cook in water bath **20 minutes at 50°C / 122°F**.
4. Pat dry. Heat pan and ghee; fry skin-side down until crisp, **1–2 minutes**. Do not fry the flesh side; serve immediately.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step; **no internal temperature stated**. The stated 50°C / 122°F is the bath temperature.
