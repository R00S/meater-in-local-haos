# Hake × Oven Roast — Recipe Temperature Research

## Cut profile

The MSC recipe uses skin-on hake fillets briefly browned in an ovenproof frying pan and then finished in a hot oven. This hybrid treatment gives the skin contact heat before the enclosed oven cooks the delicate white flesh through, while the sauce Romesco adds richness to lean hake. The published oven endpoint is “just cooked through”; no internal temperature or resting period is stated, so this leaf does not infer one.

## Source recipes

### 1. Mitch Tonks / Marine Stewardship Council — Basque-style Hake with Garlic and Sauce Romesco
**Inspired by**: https://www.msc.org/what-you-can-do/sustainable-fish-seafood-recipes/recipe/sa-basque-style-hake-with-garlic-and-sauce-romesco (fetched 2026-10-24). The page attributes the recipe to Mitch Tonks, United Kingdom, and *The SA Ocean Cookbook 2024*; its name is explicitly “Basque-style,” not a claim of a traditional Basque-authored recipe.

**Serves**: 4 · **Prep**: 20 min · **Cook**: 20 min · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 4 MSC-certified hake fillets, about 180–200 g each, skin-on
- ½ dried nora/choricero chilli; 100 ml olive oil; 4 garlic cloves, finely sliced; 20 ml agrodolce vinegar; 1 tsp chopped parsley; salt
- Romesco sauce: 4 dried nora/choricero chillies; 100 g blanched almonds; 6 garlic cloves; 12 roasted piquillo peppers; 1 tsp sweet paprika; 1 tsp smoked paprika; ¼ tsp hot smoked paprika; 25 ml sherry vinegar; 100 ml olive oil; salt

**Method**
1. Preheat the oven to 200°C. Soak the dried chillies for the fish and sauce separately for 10 minutes; drain and deseed.
2. Pulse the almonds and sauce chillies, then add garlic, peppers, paprikas, vinegar, most of the olive oil and salt; pulse to a thick Romesco sauce.
3. Heat the reserved olive oil in a large ovenproof frying pan. Fry the hake fillets skin-side down until lightly golden, then turn.
4. Transfer the pan to the oven and roast about 5 minutes, or until just cooked through.
5. Gently cook garlic and sliced chilli in a little olive oil for 2 minutes; remove from heat as the garlic begins to turn golden, cool slightly, then add vinegar and parsley.
6. Plate the hake and serve with the garlic-chilli mixture and Romesco.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step or internal temperature is stated. Published doneness cue: “just cooked through.”
