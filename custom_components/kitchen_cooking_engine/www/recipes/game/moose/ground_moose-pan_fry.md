# Minced Moose × Pan Fry — Recipe Temperature Research

## Cut profile

Pan-frying is the default method for minced moose because the meat has no fat to baste itself
with: a hot pan and a fat that is added on purpose (butter, bacon fat, cheese, cream) are the
only things standing between lean moose mince and a dry, crumbly patty. All four fetched
records build the same structure — a panade of breadcrumbs and cream (Swedish) or milk, a
binding of egg, onion and warm spice (juniper, thyme), and a two-stage cook: a hard sear for
colour, then either a lowered pan with a lid or a few minutes in a sauce. That second stage is
the method's real signature here, not a garnish: three of the four sources finish the patties
under a lid or in liquid, which is how a very lean mince is brought to a safe centre without
the surface drying out.

**Temperature evidence is the surprise of this leaf.** Not one of the four sources publishes an
internal temperature, and none of them uses a probe. They judge by colour and time — "tills de
fått fin färg", "helt gennemstegte", "hautua", "до готовности" — which means the pan-fry corpus
gives us technique and ingredient ratios but **no measurement at all**. The 71 °C / 160 °F
ground-meat target used by this cut therefore comes from the USDA rule and from the two
North American records in the `oven_bake` and `grill` leaves, not from these pages.

## Source recipes

### 1. ICA (Sweden) — Älgfärspannbiff i gräddsås med ostronskivling
**Inspired by**: https://www.ica.se/recept/algfarspannbiff-i-graddsas-med-ostronskivling-723236/ (fetched 2026-10-26). ICA (Swedish grocery chain recipe database), 4.7/5 from 26 votes. Swedish husmanskost tradition — **Culinary Group D** source.
**Serves**: 4 · **Prep**: 15 min · **Cook**: under 45 min total

**Ingredients**
- 500 g minced moose (*älgfärs*)
- ½ dl breadcrumbs · 1 dl cooking cream · 1 egg · ½ tsp salt · 1 pinch pepper
- 1 tsp thyme · 1 tbsp butter (for frying) · 150 g oyster mushrooms
- **Sauce**: 1½ dl cream; 2½ dl beef stock; 1 tbsp cornstarch; ½ tbsp soy sauce

**Method**
1. Mix breadcrumbs, cream, egg, salt and pepper; leave a few minutes to swell.
2. Work in the mince and thyme; shape into patties.
3. Brown the patties in butter until they have good colour, then lift them out.
4. Fry the sliced mushrooms in oil, season, and set aside.
5. Whisk cream, stock and cornstarch in the pan, simmer a minute, season with soy and pepper.
6. Return the patties to the sauce and **let them simmer about 8 minutes**, turning once.
7. Top with the mushrooms and chives; serve with potatoes.

*Internal temperature: not published. The page's own comment thread carries the technique note
that matters for this cut — a reader reports being told by a hunter to cut the lean moose mince
with pork mince because moose is "väldigt torrt", and the ICA kitchen agrees. The breadcrumb
quantity was corrected from 1 dl to ½ dl per 500 g mince after reader complaints, which is a
useful ratio anchor: too much panade is the common failure.*

### 2. Köket.se — Per Morbergs viltfärsbiffar
**Inspired by**: https://www.koket.se/per_morberg/varmratter/vilt/per_morbergs_viltfarsbiffar (fetched 2026-10-26). Köket.se (Swedish recipe site), Per Morberg (TV4 *Morberg*), 3.3/5 from 404 votes. Swedish chef tradition — **Culinary Group D** source.
**Serves**: 4 · **Cook**: 40 min

**Ingredients**
- 500 g game mince, ideally from moose calf (*älgkalv*)
- 3–4 tbsp breadcrumbs · 1 dl veal stock · 1½ dl whipping cream · 1 tbsp tamari soy · 2 tsp cognac · 2 eggs · 2 tbsp grated onion · 1 pinch black pepper
- Butter for frying; 1 dl blackcurrants and 4 dl game stock for the sauce

**Method**
1. Mix breadcrumbs, stock, cream, soy, cognac and eggs; leave to swell **10 minutes**.
2. Add the mince, grated onion and pepper; work to a smooth forcemeat.
3. Wet your hands and shape patties.
4. Brown butter in the pan and fry the patties **3–4 minutes per side**.
5. **Lower the heat and let them finish a few minutes.** The page's warning: do not crowd the pan, or the patties boil instead of frying.
6. Add the blackcurrants, then the game stock, and reduce; thicken if wanted and return the patties to warm through.

*Internal temperature: not published. The source argues the calf mince is the best game meat in
Swedish cooking ("liknar kalvkött från oxe"), and the cream-to-mince ratio here — 1.5 dl per
500 g — is the richest of the four.*

### 3. Webopskrifter.dk (Denmark) — Elgbøffer
**Inspired by**: https://www.webopskrifter.dk/madopskrifter/6704/ (fetched 2026-10-26). Webopskrifter.dk, recipe by Stephan Rasmussen, 2.0/5 from 1 vote. Danish home-cooking tradition — **Culinary Group D** source.
**Serves**: 4 · **Published**: 2011

**Ingredients**
- 500 g minced moose (*hakket elgkød*, *elgfars*)
- 1 egg · 200 g strong cheese, cut into 2 × 1 × 1 cm batons
- Salt and pepper

**Method**
1. Mix the mince with the egg.
2. Wrap the mince around the cheese batons into oblong patties — the cheese must be completely covered or it leaks into the pan.
3. Fry in a hot pan, seasoning with salt and pepper.
4. Once coloured on all sides, **lower the heat and put a lid on**, and leave until completely cooked through (*helt gennemstegte*).
5. Serve with redcurrant jelly, boiled potatoes, asparagus and a cream sauce with wine or sherry.

*Internal temperature: not published — the doneness cue is "helt gennemstegte" under a lid.*

### 4. Gastronom.ru (Russia) — Котлеты из лосятины (moose cutlets)
**Inspired by**: https://www.gastronom.ru/recipe/59835/kotlety-iz-losyatiny (fetched 2026-10-26). Gastronom.ru, recipe by Yulia Sinitsa, published 2025-03-22, 5/5 from 20 votes. Russian hunting-kitchen tradition — **Culinary Group D** source.
**Serves**: 6 · **Prep**: 1 h 10 min · **Cook**: 8 h incl. soaking · 320 kcal/portion

**Ingredients**
- 500 g moose meat (*лосятина*) · 150 g pork (fatty) · 120 g onion · 30 g butter · 1 potato · 2 garlic cloves
- 50 g cranberries (*клюква*) · 4 g ground cumin · 2 g ground nutmeg · black pepper, salt · 4 tbsp refined sunflower oil

**Method**
1. Soak the moose meat in cold water in the fridge for several hours, changing the water every 2–3 hours, to draw out the strong game flavour. The page treats this as the non-negotiable first step for *лосятина*.
2. Dry it; cut the moose and the pork into pieces, peel the potato and garlic.
3. Soften the diced onion in butter for 5 minutes; cool.
4. Pass meat, potato, garlic and fried onion through a coarse plate, then re-grind through a medium plate.
5. Season with cumin, nutmeg and pepper, add just under a teaspoon of salt, and beat the forcemeat by throwing it into the bowl about twenty times. Rest it an hour in the fridge. **No egg** — the page's own note says egg makes moose cutlets dense and should be avoided.
6. Shape thick round cutlets with 3–4 cranberries inside each.
7. Fry **4–5 minutes per side** in a large pan over medium-high heat until a firm brown crust forms.
8. **Add a quarter glass of water, cover, reduce the heat to low and simmer 10–15 minutes** until done.

*Internal temperature: not published; "до готовности" (until done). The step-8 covered simmer is the
same two-stage cook as the Swedish and Danish records, arrived at independently.*

## Temperature summary

| Source | Published pull temp | Doneness cue used | Tradition |
|--------|--------------------|-------------------|-----------|
| ICA (SE) | **none** | colour, then 8 min in sauce | Swedish |
| Köket / Morberg (SE) | **none** | 3–4 min/side, then lower heat | Swedish |
| Webopskrifter.dk (DK) | **none** | lid on, "helt gennemstegte" | Danish |
| Gastronom.ru (RU) | **none** | 4–5 min/side + 10–15 min covered | Russian |

**Conclusion for this leaf:** the pan-fry corpus is a technique source, not a temperature source.
Nothing here should be used to justify a pull temperature; the cut's 71 °C / 160 °F floor is
carried by the USDA ground-meat rule and by the two records that do publish a number in the
`oven_bake` and `grill` leaves. The convergent technique finding — lean moose mince is always
finished under a lid, in sauce, or after a hard sear plus lowered heat — is recorded here as the
method's own contribution.
