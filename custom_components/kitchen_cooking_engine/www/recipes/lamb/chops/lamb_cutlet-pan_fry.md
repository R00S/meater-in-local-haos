## Cut profile (method-specific)

Pan-frying a lamb cutlet is a high-heat, short-contact technique shaped entirely by the cut's thin
cross-section. A Frenched chop is 2–3 cm thick at most, with a bare rib bone running through it, so
the pan does in 2–5 minutes a side what a roast needs twenty for: the fat cap renders and crisps,
the outside browns hard, and the interior crosses from rare to well done in a window of perhaps two
minutes. That is why every fetched source uses a heavy pan (cast iron in three of five), pre-heats
it for one to three minutes, and either stands the chop on its fat cap first or presses it flat for
full contact.

Butter-and-aromatic basting — the French and British restaurant habit — is not documented by any
source fetched here; the European recipes in this leaf are oil-based, and the flavour comes from
marinades (yoghurt-spice, garlic-lemon, soy-mirin) rather than from a butter baste. Two of the five
sources give a target: **145–150 °F (63–66 °C)** in the Indian recipe and **135 °F (57 °C)**
medium-rare in the Greek one. The spread is recorded, not reconciled.

## Source recipes

### 1. Tandoori Lamb Chops — Tanvi Srivastava
**Inspired by**: Sinfully Spicy; https://sinfullyspicy.com/tandoori-lamb-chops-recipe/ (fetched 2026-10-24). Indian, tandoori-style.

**Serves**: 4 · **Prep**: 3–4 hours marinade · **Cook**: 8–10 minutes total · **Rest**: a few minutes

**Ingredients**
- 8 lamb chops (1.5 lb); 2 tbsp avocado oil for cooking
- Marinade: 2 tbsp full-fat Greek yogurt; 1 tbsp lemon juice; 1 tbsp avocado oil; ½ tbsp garlic paste; ½ tsp ginger paste; 2 tbsp chopped cilantro and mint; 1¼ tsp salt
- Spices: 1½ tsp coriander powder; ½ tsp cumin; 1 tsp red chilli powder; ¼ tsp black pepper; ¼ tsp cinnamon; ⅛ tsp turmeric; ¼ tsp chaat masala

**Method**
1. Whisk the marinade smooth, coat the chops and refrigerate 3–4 hours (overnight is better); bring out 30–40 minutes before cooking.
2. Heat a 10-inch cast-iron skillet on high and add 1½ tbsp oil; cook the chops in batches.
3. Pan-sear until golden on one side, turn with tongs and brown the other side — "typically takes 8 to 10 minutes in total."
4. "Use a thermometer and make sure that each lamb chop reaches an internal temperature of 145°F - 150°F when probed at its thickest part."
5. Rest a few minutes before serving.

**Probe and endpoint**: explicit probe instruction — 145–150 °F (63–66 °C) at the thickest part, which brackets the cut's 63 °C safe line at its lower end.

### 2. Dad's Pan-Fried Lamb Chops: The Chinese Restaurant Way — Daddy Lau
**Inspired by**: Made With Lau; https://www.madewithlau.com/recipes/lamb-chops (fetched 2026-10-24). Cantonese Chinese.

**Serves**: 4 · **Prep**: 1–2 hours marinate (optional) · **Cook**: 5–6 minutes · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 1 tsp salt; 20 oz lamb (rack, separated into chops)
- 1 oz garlic; 1 oz white onion; 1 oz red onion; 2 oz red bell pepper
- ½ tbsp corn oil; 1 tbsp butter; 1 tbsp oyster sauce; 1 tbsp cooking wine; ½ tsp garlic salt
- Seasoning: 1 tsp black pepper; 1 tsp white pepper; 2 tsp garlic salt; 1 tbsp olive oil

**Method**
1. Soak the rack in salted water 1–2 minutes, pat dry, separate into chops and tenderise both sides with the back of a knife.
2. Season both sides and drizzle with oil; marinate 1–2 hours if time allows.
3. Heat the pan on high for 2 minutes, add the corn oil, and stand the chops on their fat caps for 1 minute.
4. Fry one side 1–2 minutes until golden, then the other side for 1 minute. Add butter, turn the heat to low and fry a final 30 seconds; remove the chops.
5. Fry the garlic, then the onions and pepper in the rendered fat; add the oyster sauce and wine.
6. Return the chops with their juices and fry 30 seconds; plate.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step and no internal temperature. Published cue: "until golden brown", with a 1-2-1 minute sequence plus a 30-second butter finish.

### 3. Pirzola: How to Prepare Succulent Lamb Chops — Bodrum
**Inspired by**: Bodrum (New York restaurant); https://www.bodrumnyc.com/blog/pirzola (fetched 2026-10-24). Turkish.

**Serves**: 4 · **Prep**: 20 minutes · **Cook**: 6–8 minutes · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 8 extra-thin lamb chops
- Olive oil; salt; black pepper
- 5–6 sprigs thyme; 2 cloves garlic; 2 sprigs mint; ½ cup fresh parsley; lemony sumac seasoning
- Small potatoes and vegetables, to serve

**Method**
1. Roast the sliced vegetables and potatoes at 350 °F (175 °C) for about an hour — this is the side, not the lamb.
2. Purée the herbs and seasonings into a rub and work it into the chops.
3. Heat a skillet over high heat for one minute, coat the chops lightly with salt, pepper and olive oil and lay them in.
4. Cook 3–4 minutes, flip, and repeat on the other side.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step and no internal temperature. The page explicitly warns against piercing the chops with a knife or fork to check them, and gives no thermometer alternative — thin chops are judged by feel and time.

### 4. Pan-Fried Lamb Chops with Mint Vinaigrette — Irena Macri
**Inspired by**: Cooked & Loved; https://www.cookedandloved.com/recipes/pan-fried-lamb-chops-with-mint-vinaigrette/ (fetched 2026-10-24). Australian (recipe card cuisine "Australian").

**Serves**: 4 · **Prep**: 10 minutes · **Cook**: 8–9 minutes · **Rest**: 1–2 minutes

**Ingredients**
- 10–12 lamb chops, cutlets or rib chops
- 1 tsp salt; ½ tsp black pepper; ½ tsp garlic powder; ½ tsp cumin; ½ tsp sweet paprika; ½ tsp coriander seed powder; pinch chilli
- Oil or ghee for cooking
- Mint vinaigrette: 15 mint leaves; ¼ red onion; 1 tsp chopped mild red chilli; 1 tbsp capers; 1 tsp caper brine; 1½ tbsp lemon juice; 3 tbsp olive oil

**Method**
1. Season both sides with the salt, pepper and spices (can be done hours ahead).
2. Heat 1–2 tbsp oil or ghee in a large frying pan over medium-high until sizzling.
3. Cook 5 minutes on the first side and 3–4 minutes on the second. Rest 1–2 minutes.
4. Chop and combine the vinaigrette ingredients and serve alongside.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step and no internal temperature; the source gives timing and a short rest only.

### 5. Pan-Fried Lamb Chops — Vered DeLeeuw
**Inspired by**: Healthy Recipes Blog; https://healthyrecipesblogs.com/lamb-chops/ (fetched 2026-10-24). Greek.

**Serves**: 3 · **Prep**: 5 minutes · **Cook**: 4–6 minutes · **Rest**: 5 minutes

**Ingredients**
- 6 single-rib lamb chops, about ½ inch thick
- ½ tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt; ¼ tsp black pepper; ½ tsp garlic powder; ½ tsp dried rosemary or thyme
- 1 tbsp ghee

**Method**
1. Sprinkle the chops with the salt, pepper, garlic powder and rosemary.
2. Heat a large cast-iron skillet over high heat about 3 minutes; add the ghee and brush to coat.
3. Add the chops and cook 2–3 minutes per side, "aiming for an internal temperature of 135℉ (medium-rare)". For chops thicker than ½ inch, cook the edges about 1 minute, especially the fat edge.
4. Rest loosely covered 5 minutes.

**Probe and endpoint**: states a 135 °F (57 °C) medium-rare target and separately notes the CDC recommendation of 145 °F with a 3-minute rest; the method itself does not instruct using a thermometer.

| Made With Lau, Bodrum, Cooked & Loved | none stated | Chinese, Turkish, Australian |

Two published targets 10–15 °F apart, and three sources that rely entirely on time and colour. The
spread is a doneness preference; both figures are recorded as published.

## What makes this method special

A hot pan is the only equipment a cutlet really needs, and it gives something the grill cannot:
the rendered fat stays in the pan and bastes the chop from below while the fat cap crisps against
cast iron. The cut's bone also acts as a heat sink, which is why the Cantonese source stands the
chops on their fat caps first and why the Greek source cooks the fat edge separately on thick
chops. The method's weakness is that everything happens in five minutes: the marinade has to carry
the flavour, because there is no time to build one in the pan.

## Summary

Pan-fried lamb cutlets cook in 2–5 minutes a side in a pre-heated heavy pan, usually after a
yoghurt, spice or citrus marinade, with published targets of 135 °F and 145–150 °F. Five fetched
sources across Cantonese, Indian, Turkish, Australian and Greek practice; two state a temperature.
