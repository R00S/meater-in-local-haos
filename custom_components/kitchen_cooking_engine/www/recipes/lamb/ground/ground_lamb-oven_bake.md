## Cut profile

Ground lamb's second great life is as the savoury layer inside baked dishes — moussaka,
kibbeh bil sanieh, shepherd's pie — where the mince is first browned and simmered on the
stovetop, then baked inside a casserole (béchamel, bulgur crust, mashed potato). The oven
phase is not where the meat cooks: it is where the assembled dish sets and the meat sauce's
flavours meld under a topping. This two-stage structure is why none of these traditions
measures the meat's internal temperature — by the time the casserole bakes, the mince has
long passed every safe threshold. The one fetched source is Greek moussaka; a Turkish
kıymalı patates record (previously misfiled under pan_fry) is retained below as a lead.

## Source recipes

### 1. Traditional Greek Moussaka with Eggplant — Suzy Karadsheh / The Mediterranean Dish
**Inspired by**: https://www.themediterraneandish.com/moussaka-recipe-eggplant-casserole/ (fetched 2026-10-24). Suzy Karadsheh, The Mediterranean Dish; Greek tradition. Ingredient line: "1 pound ground lamb or beef" (both permitted, as published).

**Serves**: 12 pieces · **Prep**: 30 min · **Cook**: 1 h 30 min · **Rest**: 10 min before slicing

**Ingredients**
*Eggplant*
- 2 large globe eggplants
- Kosher salt
- Extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/4 cup breadcrumbs

*Meat sauce*
- 1 large yellow onion, finely chopped
- 1 pound ground lamb or beef
- 1 tablespoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika
- 1/2 cup red wine
- 1 (14-ounce) can diced tomatoes
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 1/2 cup beef broth, warmed until simmering

*Béchamel*
- 1/3 cup + 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 2/3 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
- Black pepper, to taste
- 4 cups 2% milk, warmed
- 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 2 large eggs

**Method**
1. Trim and slice the eggplant into 1/4-inch slabs; salt in one layer and leave 30 minutes.
2. Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a large skillet over **medium heat**. Cook the onion until slightly golden, about 5 minutes.
3. **Add the ground lamb and brown it. Pour off excess fat.**
4. Add the oregano, cinnamon, pepper, nutmeg, paprika and a pinch of salt. Add the wine and boil 1 minute; stir in the tomatoes, sugar and hot broth. **Simmer over medium-low heat 20–30 minutes.**
5. Turn the oven broiler to high. Pat the eggplant dry, brush with olive oil and broil in a single layer, turning, until softened and golden (about 6 inches from the element).
6. Turn off the broiler and preheat the oven to **350°F**. Oil a baking dish.
7. Make the béchamel: cook the olive oil with flour, salt and pepper until golden; gradually whisk in the warm milk and cook 5–7 minutes until thickened. Add the nutmeg.
8. Temper the eggs with a ladleful of hot béchamel, whisk back into the sauce, bring to a gentle boil 2 minutes, then remove from heat.
9. Layer half the eggplant in the dish, add the meat sauce, then the remaining eggplant. Top with the béchamel and breadcrumbs.
10. **Bake on the centre rack 45 minutes.** (Broil briefly afterwards for a more golden top if desired.)
11. Let stand **10 minutes** before slicing.

**Probe and endpoint**: no probe step. **no internal temperature stated** — the mince is browned and simmered 20–30 min before baking; the endpoint is the casserole's 45-minute bake and 10-minute set.

## What makes this method special

Baking turns ground lamb from a standalone preparation into a layer: the browned, spiced meat
sauce (wine, cinnamon, oregano — the Greek canon) sits under béchamel or another topping and
bakes into a sliceable casserole. The mince's fat keeps the sauce loose under the crust, and
the earlier browning-plus-simmer is where the actual meat cookery happens — which is exactly
why no baked-dish tradition measures the meat. For this cut, oven baking is less a cooking
method for the lamb than a finishing method for a dish built on it.

## Summary

Oven baking gives ground lamb its casserole life — browned, spiced meat sauce set under béchamel in the Greek canon.
