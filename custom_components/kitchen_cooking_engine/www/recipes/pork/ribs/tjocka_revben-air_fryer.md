# Tjocka Revben × Air Fryer — Recipe Temperature Research

## Cut profile

An air fryer is a small convection oven: violently moving dry heat browns the surface and renders the fat of a meat-covered rack far faster than an oven does, which is exactly why every source below manages the thick cover by wrapping or lowering the temperature partway through rather than holding one setting. The cut's own thickness is the risk here — the German source warns that air-fryer ribs do not fall off the bone, and the Chinese source's advice is to add time when the rack carries a thick layer of meat — so the method suits portioned or cracked pieces better than an unsplit front rack. Four traditions are represented — Chinese, German, Brazilian and Korean — and the Korean source is the only one that names the cut precisely (쪽갈비). No source in any language states a core temperature; every °C figure below is a machine set-point, and the four set-points differ (180, 190, 200 °C), which is the real finding for this method. Carryover is not measured by any source, but a small chamber recovers heat quickly after the basket opens, so it should not be assumed to be zero.

## Styckesprofil

En airfryer är en liten varmluftsugn: kraftigt rörig, torr värme bräner ytan och smälter fettet i ett kötrikt spjäll snabbare än en ugn gör, och just därför hanterar varje källa det tjocka köttlagret genom att svepa in eller sänka tempot halvvägs — ingen håller ett enda program hela tiden. Styckets ojämna tjocklek är risken: den tyska källan varnar för att airfryer-ribben inte lossar från benet, och den kinesiska källans råd är att lägga till tid när spjället har ett tjockt lager kött. Fyra traditioner är representerade — kinesisk, tysk, brasiliansk och koreansk — och den koreanska källan är den enda som namnger stycket exakt (쪽갈비). Ingen källa anger kärntemperatur på något språk; varje °C-figur nedan är ett maskinprogram, och de fyra programmen skiljer sig (180, 190, 200 °C) — det är den egentliga iakttagelsen för metoden. Eftervärmen mäts inte av någon källa.

## Source recipes

### 1. Chinese (美食天下, 阳光明媚99) — 酱香烤排骨【空气炸锅版】
**Inspired by**: 阳光明媚99, 美食天下; https://home.meishichina.com/recipe-352536.html (fetched 2026-11-01). *Named home-cook author, posted as 独家 (exclusive). Cut term is generic 排骨; the page is written around a specific machine (九阳 KL-50G1) and states its default program, so the machine settings below are that appliance's, not a universal time.*
**Serves**: not stated (600 g ribs) · **Prep**: overnight marinating · **Cook**: 25 min · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 排骨 600 克 — 600 g pork ribs
- 姜 10 克 — 10 g ginger
- 干辣椒 5 克 — 5 g dried chilli
- 盐 3 克 — 3 g salt
- 十三香 4 克 — 4 g *shísān xiāng* (Chinese thirteen-spice powder)
- 葱 20 克 — 20 g scallion
- 蒜 10 克 — 10 g garlic
- 烤肉酱 适量 — barbecue sauce, to taste (适量)
- 橄榄油 10 克 — 10 g olive oil
- 料酒 10 克 — 10 g Chinese cooking wine

**Method**
1. Wash the ribs; slice the scallion, chilli, ginger and garlic.
2. Combine the aromatics with the drained ribs, then add the cooking wine, olive oil and barbecue sauce and mix.
3. Prick the ribs all over with a bamboo skewer so the marinade penetrates; cover with film and marinate **overnight** in the refrigerator.
4. Set the air fryer's meat-rib program (**default: 200 °C, 25 minutes**) and load the ribs. Insert the MEATER probe from the side into the thickest piece, away from bone, before the cycle starts.
5. Close the lid and start; the source notes that on this machine the visible window shows the ribs rendering fat.
6. **Pull temperature: not stated by source.** The only temperature on the page, 200 °C, is the appliance set-point; no core temperature is printed and none is inferred.
7. Final serving temperature: **not stated by source** — 25 minutes, and the ribs are out.

### 2. German (tastybits.de, Jannik Degner) — BBQ-Spareribs aus dem Airfryer
**Inspired by**: Jannik Degner, tastybits.de; https://www.tastybits.de/rezepte/fleisch-gefluegel/spareribs-aus-der-heissluftfritteuse/ (fetched 2026-11-01). *Named author ("gekocht & geschrieben von Jannik Degner"). Cut term: Spareribs, with the author advising the buyer to ask for the St. Louis Cut — a trimmed belly-side rack, so a comparator rather than this cut.*
**Serves**: 2 · **Prep**: 3 h marinating (or overnight) · **Cook**: ca 1 h 55 min · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- Spareribs 750 g (author advises asking the butcher for the St. Louis Cut)
- **Dry rub**: 1 tsp each salt, black pepper, sweet paprika; ½ tsp each cumin, onion powder, garlic powder, brown sugar; 1 tsp chilli powder if liked — or an equal amount of a ready rub
- Apfelsaft — a good splash of apple juice
- BBQ sauce — to taste, for glazing

**Method**
1. Lift the membrane off the bone side with the handle of a spoon and peel it away.
2. Pound the rub ingredients together in a mortar and press the rub firmly into the meat on all sides.
3. Wrap in cling film and rest in the refrigerator **3 hours** (overnight is fine).
4. Spread the ribs in the air-fryer basket and cook **10 minutes at 180 °C**. Insert the MEATER probe from the side into the thickest piece, away from bone, at the start.
5. Lay the ribs on baking paper, add a good splash of apple juice, wrap the parcel (a second sheet of paper around it to keep liquid in), and cook **90 minutes at 150 °C**.
6. Unwrap, brush with BBQ sauce, return to the basket with the pieces not touching, and cook **5–10 minutes at 200 °C** until crisp and sticky.
7. **Pull temperature: not stated by source.** All three figures on this page (180/150/200 °C) are appliance set-points; no core temperature appears. The author's own verdict is honest and worth keeping: *"Das Fleisch fällt nicht so vom Knochen"* — the meat does not fall off the bone this way.
8. Final serving temperature: **not stated by source** — serve with the sides of your choice.

### 3. Brazilian (TudoGostoso) — Costelinha de porco na airfryer
**Inspired by**: TudoGostoso (named Brazilian food-media outlet, site test-kitchen recipe); https://www.tudogostoso.com.br/receita/317544-costelinha-de-porco-na-airfryer.html (fetched 2026-11-01). *Content verified against the returned page — this domain is listed in `SOURCE_SURVEY.md` as having a wrong-content-ID trap, and the fetched body matched the recipe title and steps. Cut term is costelinha (portioned rack), not a positioned front/back term.*
**Serves**: not stated (1 kg ribs) · **Prep**: 1 h marinating · **Cook**: 35 min (10 min preheat + 25 min) · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 1 kg de costelinha suína — 1 kg pork ribs
- Suco de 1 limão — juice of 1 lime
- 1 colher (chá) de pimenta-do-reino — 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 colher (sopa) de açúcar mascavo — 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 colher (sopa) páprica defumada — 1 tbsp smoked paprika
- 4 dentes de alho amassados — 4 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 colher (sopa) de sal — 1 tbsp salt

**Method**
1. Put the ribs in a bowl.
2. Add the lime juice, pepper, brown sugar, smoked paprika, garlic and salt.
3. Mix everything thoroughly.
4. Marinate **1 hour**.
5. Preheat the air fryer **10 minutes**.
6. Put the ribs in and cook **25 minutes at 180 °C**, turning the piece halfway through for even cooking, as the source's tip directs. Insert the MEATER probe from the side into the thickest piece, away from bone, when the ribs go in.
7. **Pull temperature: not stated by source.** The 180 °C figure is the appliance set-point; the page prints no core temperature.
8. Final serving temperature: **not stated by source** — the source serves it as a snack with lemon and beer.

### 4. Korean (Naver blog, 테체후) — 양념 쪽갈비 에어프라이어 구이
**Inspired by**: 테체후 (food influencer, Naver 少女食卓 blog); https://m.blog.naver.com/ssiulian/224345611598 (fetched 2026-11-01). *Named Korean blogger, writing in Korean; page discloses a brand-Connect affiliate relationship. **The cut term is 쪽갈비** — the front, belly-adjacent rib — which makes this the only source in this leaf that names this cut's region of the rack.*
**Serves**: not stated · **Prep**: not stated (store-bought marinated ribs) · **Cook**: ca 27 min · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 양념 쪽갈비 — pre-marinated seasoned *jjok-galbi* (front rib), quantity not stated on the page (the linked product is a 550 g pack; the source does not say how much it used)
- 종이호일 — parchment liner
- 후추, 파슬리 가루 — pepper and parsley, to finish

**Method**
1. Line the air-fryer basket with parchment, cutting a small **+** in the centre so the paper settles without tearing — the source's reason is that the ribs are already marinated and the marinade must not run off before the meat cooks.
2. Lay the ribs **meaty side up** and preheat/run at **190 °C for 15 minutes**; the source states plainly that it does not go above 190 °C because *양념이 탈 수 있어서* — the marinade burns. Insert the MEATER probe from the side into the thickest piece, away from bone, at the start.
3. Turn the pieces onto their sides, focusing on the meaty side, for about **7 minutes**.
4. Flip completely so the **bone side is up** and cook **5 minutes** more.
5. Plate, finish with pepper and parsley. The source's safety note: drain the fat a third at a time when removing the basket, since tipping it fully can throw hot fat.
6. **Pull temperature: not stated by source.** 190 °C is the set-point; no core temperature appears.
7. Final serving temperature: **not stated by source**.
