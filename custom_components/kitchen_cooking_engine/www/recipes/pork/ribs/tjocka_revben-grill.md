# Tjocka Revben × Grill — Recipe Temperature Research

## Cut profile

Grilling exposes the meat-covered front pork ribs to direct or indirect heat. The cut-exact Swedish recipe grills individual ribs with a coconut marinade; the other retained recipes use ribs whose exact correspondence is uncertain. Thick and thin sections can cook differently, and fat dripping over fire can cause flare-ups. The sources do not quantify carryover.

## Source recipes

### 1. Swedish (Svenskt Kött) — Grillade tjocka spjäll med jordnötssås
**Inspired by**: Svenskt Kött; https://svensktkott.se/recept/cocos-barbecue-spjll/ (fetched 2026-10-31). *Coconut-Asian marinade; the source states the ribs are "lika goda att grilla i ugn som på utegrill" — equally good grilled in the oven or outdoors; high heat, short time.*
**Serves**: 6 · **Prep**: 10 min + 2 h marinating · **Cook**: ca 15 min · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- ca 1 kg tjocka revbensspjäll, **cut into individual ribs**
- 1 tsk salt
- **Marinad**: 1 dl kokosmjölk (coconut milk), 1 msk soja, 2 vitlöksklyftor (garlic cloves), hackade, ½ msk koriander (coriander), hackad, 1 msk farinsocker (caster sugar), 1 tsk sambal oelek, 1 msk färsk ingefära (fresh ginger), riven
- **Jordnötssås**: 1 dl jordnötssmör (peanut butter), 1 dl kokosmjölk, 1 dl salsa eller tacosås, ½ citron rivet skal (lemon zest), 1 krm cayennepeppar
- To serve: grilled pineapple rings, rice

**Method**
1. Rub the ribs with salt. Mix the marinade ingredients; add the ribs and marinate **2 hours** in the fridge.
2. Make the peanut sauce: heat peanut butter, coconut milk and salsa in a saucepan, add the spices, bring to the boil; set aside for serving.
3. Grill the ribs about **15 minutes** at **275 °C** in the oven or on the outdoor grill, turning now and then; insert the MEATER probe from the side into the thickest rib, away from bone, at the start. Grill the pineapple rings about 1 minute per side.
4. **Pull temperature: not stated by source.** Use the source’s time and texture cues; do not treat an estimated range as a measured recipe target.
5. Final serving temperature: **not stated by source** — serve with rice, peanut sauce and grilled pineapple.

### 2. Finnish (Gloria ruoka ja viini, chef Jouni Toivonen) — Todella hitaat ribsit
**Inspired by**: Jouni Toivonen, published on Glorian ruoka ja viini via Soppa365; https://www.soppa365.fi/reseptit/liha-grillaus/todella-hitaat-ribsit (fetched 2026-10-31). *"Really slow ribs" — marinate, braise in cola and apple juice, then blast on the grill. 12 h total.*
**Serves**: 4–6 · **Prep**: 30 min + marinating · **Cook**: ca 2 h braise + 10 min grill · **Rest**: 20 min in the broth

**Ingredients**
- 1 kg ribsejä — porsaan kylkiluita rivinä (pork ribs in a rack)
- 2 valkosipulinkynttä (garlic cloves), 1 rkl suolaa (salt), 1 rkl ruokosokeria (granulated sugar), 1 tl chilijauhetta (chilli powder), 1 tl paprikajauhetta (paprika), ¼ tl mustapippuria (black pepper)
- 1 pieni pullo (33 cl) kolajuomaa (cola), ½ l omenamehua (apple juice), 1 limetin mehu (lime juice)
- 1 punainen chili, 2 rkl tuoretta korianteria (coriander), to finish

**Method**
1. Rub the ribs with the spice mix; refrigerate **at least 2 hours, preferably overnight**.
2. Cut the racks to fit a pot; pour in the cola and apple juice; cover and braise on low heat **2 hours**.
3. Let the ribs rest in the liquid **20 minutes**; remove the meat; boil the broth down to about **1 dl** of thick sauce.
4. Heat the grill to **full power**. Insert the MEATER probe from the side into the thickest part, away from bone. Brush the ribs with the reduced sauce and grill **5 minutes per side**, turning occasionally; glaze with the remaining sauce at the end.
5. **Pull temperature: not stated by source.** Use the source’s time and texture cues; do not treat an estimated range as a measured recipe target.
6. Final serving temperature: **not stated by source** — squeeze lime over the ribs and scatter fresh chilli and coriander before serving.

### 3. South African (Taste Magazine SA, Strone & Ashleigh Henry) — Cherry-and-ginger BBQ spareribs
**Inspired by**: Strone and Ashleigh Henry, Taste Magazine (South Africa); https://taste.co.za/recipes/cherry-and-ginger-bbq-spareribs/ (fetched 2026-10-31). *Explicitly "pork belly spareribs racks" — the closest identity match to tjocka revben outside Sweden; simmered in ginger beer, then braaied.*
**Serves**: 4–6 · **Prep**: 10 min · **Cook**: 1 h simmer + 10–15 min grill · **Rest**: briefly

**Ingredients**
- 2 × 1.2–1.8 kg **pork belly spareribs racks**
- 2 L ginger beer, 2 t salt
- **Cherry BBQ sauce**: 300 g fresh or frozen stoned cherries, 3 T brown sugar, 2 T red wine vinegar, 1 T fresh grated ginger, 1 cup smoky braai marinade
- Grilled nectarine salad (to serve): 4–6 nectarines, olive oil, lemon juice, 40 g wild rocket, 1 small red onion

**Method**
1. Place the ribs in a large deep saucepan with the ginger beer and salt; bring to the boil, then simmer gently **1 hour covered** until tender but still holding together. Remove and cool slightly; pat dry if needed.
2. Make the cherry sauce: cook cherries, sugar, vinegar and ginger 10 minutes until the cherries break down; stir in the braai marinade and simmer a further 5–10 minutes until thickened; blend if desired.
3. Prepare a braai (or heat the oven grill to medium-high). Insert the MEATER probe from the side into the thickest part of a rack, away from bone. Baste the ribs generously with cherry sauce and grill or roast **10–15 minutes**, turning and basting often, until caramelised and sticky.
4. **Pull temperature: not stated by source.** Use the source’s time and texture cues; do not treat an estimated range as a measured recipe target.
5. Final serving temperature: **not stated by source** — rest briefly, slice between the bones, serve with the grilled nectarine salad.

### 4. British (BBC Good Food, Good Food team) — Sizzling spare ribs with BBQ sauce
**Inspired by**: BBC Good Food; https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/sizzling-spare-ribs-bbq-sauce (archived copy fetched 2026-10-31). *Caribbean-influenced British recipe — rum, Scotch bonnet, allspice — and the only fetched grill source that puts RAW marinated ribs straight on a moderate barbecue. Rated 5/5, 49 ratings.*
**Serves**: 6 · **Prep**: 10 min + marinating · **Cook**: 20–30 min · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 4 × 500 g packs pork spare ribs
- 1 bunch spring onions, roughly chopped (green stems and all)
- 1 Scotch bonnet chilli, seeded and finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, roughly chopped
- 6 tbsp Appleton rum, 6 tbsp demerara sugar, 6 tbsp dark soy sauce, 6 tbsp clear honey, 6 tsp Dijon mustard, 1 tsp ground allspice
- Salt and pepper

**Method**
1. Tip the ribs into a large (non-metal) bowl with the spring onions, chilli and garlic; spoon over all the remaining ingredients and turn the ribs over and over until coated. The source notes the uncooked ribs keep tightly covered in the fridge up to 3 days and can be frozen.
2. Cook the ribs over a **moderate barbecue for 20–30 minutes** depending on size, turning frequently and brushing with sauce each time — the turning and brushing builds the lacquer on all four sides of each rib.
3. Insert the MEATER probe from the side into the thickest rib while grilling, away from bone; the ribs are turned so often that the probe leads can be routed along the grid edge.
4. **Pull temperature: not stated by source.** Use the source’s time and texture cues; do not treat an estimated range as a measured recipe target.
5. Final serving temperature: **not stated by source** — heat any leftover sauce in a pan and pour over the ribs just before serving.

### 5. Thai (Simply Suwanee) — ซี่โครงหมูย่าง / Thai grilled BBQ pork spare ribs
**Inspired by**: Simply Suwanee; https://www.simplysuwanee.com/thai-pork-spare-ribs/ (read 2026-11-01). *Thai marinade of fish sauce, oyster sauce, sweet dark soy, garlic and coriander root; grilled medium-low and the only Thai rib recipe found that states a numeric internal temperature.*
**Serves**: 4 · **Prep**: 20 min + 4 h or overnight · **Cook**: 50–60 min · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 3 lb pork spare ribs (the source recommends St Louis-style or loin back ribs)
- ½–1 tsp ground white pepper; 2 tbsp roughly chopped garlic; ¼ cup minced coriander stems (or 3 coriander roots)
- 2 tbsp fish sauce; 3 tbsp oyster sauce; 3 tbsp sweet dark soy sauce; 1 tbsp sugar; 2 tsp lime juice or rice vinegar
- 2 tsp oil, for brushing the grill

**Method**
1. Pound or blend the fish sauce, oyster sauce, sweet dark soy, sugar, garlic, coriander, white pepper and lime juice into a rough paste.
2. Separate the racks if needed, wash and pat dry, and remove the membrane. Coat the ribs in the marinade, cover and refrigerate at least **4 hours or overnight**.
3. Turn the ribs to recoat and sit at room temperature 30–45 minutes. Preheat the grill to medium-low 10 minutes ahead.
4. Grill over medium-low, turning and basting with the reserved marinade; move darkening pieces to indirect heat.
5. Insert the MEATER probe from the side into the thickest rib, away from bone, once the surface has set.
6. **Pull at 165 °F (74 °C)** — the source's stated internal temperature, reached after about **25–30 minutes per side**.
7. Final serving temperature: **not stated by source** — serve hot with sticky rice and nam jim jaew.
