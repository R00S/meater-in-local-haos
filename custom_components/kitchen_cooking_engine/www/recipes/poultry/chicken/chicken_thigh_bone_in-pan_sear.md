# Chicken Thigh (Bone-in) × Pan Sear — Recipe Temperature Research

## Cut profile

Pan-searing bone-in thighs uses high skillet heat to crisp and brown skin, then continues cooking covered in the same pan with a small amount of broth. Unlike the pan-roast leaf, this fetched recipe remains on the stovetop throughout; unlike a quick pan-fry, it includes a covered moist finish for the bone-in centre. The method is a hybrid sear-and-pan-braise, with the source relying on a thermometer rather than time alone.

## Source recipe

### Julie Maestre / Cooked by Julie — Pan-Seared Chicken Thighs
**Inspired by**: https://www.cookedbyjulie.com/pan-seared-chicken-thighs/ (fetched 2026-10-24). US home recipe; exact cut: “4 chicken thighs, bone-in skin-on.”

**Serves**: 4 · **Prep**: 5 min · **Cook**: 24 min · **Rest**: not stated

**Ingredients**
- 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs; 2 tbsp canola or vegetable oil
- 1 tsp garlic powder; 1 tsp paprika; 1 tsp onion powder; 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning; salt to taste
- 1/2 lemon; 2 garlic cloves, minced; 1 cup chicken stock; 1 tbsp butter

**Method**
1. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Combine paprika, garlic powder, onion powder and Italian seasoning.
2. Season chicken under the skin with salt and spice blend; season outer skin with salt only.
3. Place skin-side down and cook 7 minutes; flip and cook 7 minutes more. Remove and set aside.
4. Discard excess oil. Add butter and garlic; cook 15 seconds. Add stock and lemon juice; bring to a simmer.
5. Return chicken, cover and cook 10 minutes or until fully cooked through. The author recommends cooking to **165°F internal** with thermometer.

**Probe and endpoint**: use meat thermometer and cook to 165°F; exact probe placement is not specified.

## Method distinction

The source names pan-searing, uses a high-heat skin-side start and then a covered broth finish; it does not use an oven. This is one credible cut × method example, but it overlaps technically with pan-fry and braise, so the classification remains a documented lead rather than a complete separate-method consensus.

## Summary

A fetched recipe directly identifies bone-in, skin-on thighs as pan-seared and gives a 7+7-minute sear followed by a 10-minute covered finish to 165°F. **PARTIAL RESEARCH**: one US source; additional traditions are needed to establish a distinct pan-sear leaf.
