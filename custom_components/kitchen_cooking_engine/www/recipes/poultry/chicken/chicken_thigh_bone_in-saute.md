# Chicken Thigh (Bone-in) × Sauté — Recipe Temperature Research

## Cut profile

Sautéing bone-in thighs uses an uncovered hot-fat start to brown the skin, then the source recipe lowers heat and covers the pan for a slower finish. Although the technique begins with a dry-heat sauté, the final covered stage makes this a hybrid sauté-and-pan-braise, not a pure quick sauté. This distinction is preserved rather than collapsing it into the separate pan-fry leaf.

## Source recipe

### Susan S. Bradley / LunaCafe — Sautéed Chicken Thighs with Savory Herbs & Garlic à la Julia Child
**Inspired by**: https://www.thelunacafe.com/sauteed-chicken-thighs-savory-herbs-garlic-ala-julia-child/ (fetched 2026-10-24). French-inspired recipe adapted from Julia Child’s *Mastering the Art of French Cooking*; cut is “8-10 plump, bone-in chicken thighs (about 4 pounds).”

**Serves**: 4–6 · **Prep**: optional 1–3 day dry cure · **Cook**: about 35 min in skillet, plus sauce · **Rest**: held warm in 300°F oven

**Ingredients**
- Chicken: 4 tbsp clarified butter (or half butter and half cooking oil); 8–10 bone-in chicken thighs (about 4 lb)
- Seasoning: 1 1/2 tsp dried thyme; 1 1/2 tsp dried basil; 1 1/2 tsp fennel, ground; fine sea salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste; 4 garlic cloves, minced
- Sauce: 1/2 cup dry white wine; 1 large egg yolk; 1/4 cup heavy cream; 1 tbsp lemon juice; 2 tbsp unsalted butter
- Garnish: 1 tbsp minced fresh parsley
- Optional dry cure: sea salt, black pepper and same measured herbs (recipe gives method but no separate quantities)

**Method**
1. Pat thighs thoroughly dry. If dry-curing, season with salt, pepper and herbs, wrap and refrigerate 1–3 days.
2. Heat clarified butter in a large skillet over medium-high until fully foaming. Add thighs skin-side down without crowding; crush herbs over them and season.
3. After about 10 minutes, check skin; turn pieces when golden brown.
4. Add minced garlic, cover skillet, lower heat to medium-low and cook slowly another 25 minutes, or until juices run clear at the bone. The page says an instant-read thermometer should read **180°F**.
5. Transfer chicken to a heated platter and hold warm in a 300°F oven.
6. Raise skillet heat, add wine and reduce by half. Whisk egg yolk and cream, temper with a little pan liquid and return to skillet; do not let sauce simmer. Season with salt, pepper and lemon; finish with butter and parsley.

**Probe and endpoint**: the source says instant-read thermometer **180°F**, with juices running clear at bone as the visual cue. It does not specify exact probe placement.
